# Transfer service fees

> Sample content for the demo; the institution and providers are fictional.

## RemitFast (simulated) <!-- clause: remitfast-fees -->

RemitFast is the one simulated transfer service in this sandbox. It converts MYR to CNY. It charges a fixed fee of MYR 8.00 per transfer and an exchange-rate markup of 1.2% against the mid-market rate. Money arrives in about 4 hours.

## How Bridge shows the cost <!-- clause: true-cost -->

For every conversion Bridge shows the true cost: the fixed fee plus what the exchange-rate markup costs, compared with the mid-market rate. Bridge does not compare transfer services.
