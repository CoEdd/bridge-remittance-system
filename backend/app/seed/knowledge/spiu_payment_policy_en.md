# Shenzhen Polytechnic International University: paying tuition and fees

> Sample content for the demo; the institution and providers are fictional.

## Where to pay <!-- clause: official-channel -->

Pay tuition and fees only to the University's official account printed on your invoice, or through the student portal. Do not pay into any other account.

## No payment agents and no discounts <!-- clause: no-agents-no-discounts -->

The University never uses payment agents and never offers tuition discounts through third parties. Anyone who offers to pay your fees for you at a lower price does not act for the University.

## No threats about your visa <!-- clause: no-visa-threats -->

The University never threatens a student's visa status over fees. If a fee is late, the Finance Office writes to your university email and applies the late fee shown on the invoice.

## Contacting the Finance Office <!-- clause: contact-finance-office -->

If you are unsure about a payment request, ask the Finance Office before you pay: finance.office@spiu.example, or in person at the Finance Office on campus.
